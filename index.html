Garchomple runs Pokémon White 2 (or Black 2) in your browser. Pick your ROM file once; it's remembered in this browser, so next time it's one click. Everything else, including a postgame save, is built in.

Your ROM stays in this browser (IndexedDB). It is never uploaded anywhere.

Teams built from PokeAPI data via pokebase, rolled back to Black 2 / White 2 rules. Emulation by EmulatorJS + melonDS. Fan project, not affiliated with Nintendo, Game Freak or The Pokémon Company.

Developed by Adi Balaji with the help of generative AI.

How to play

  1. Daily: every day everyone gets the same random six: fully evolved Gen 1–5 Pokémon with max IVs, 252/252 EVs, a fitting nature, item and moveset. Five are level 76 and the ace is level 78, the same as Cynthia's team.
  2. Press Battle Cynthia. The game opens in Undella Town within seconds, walks you into her villa and accepts her challenge. (The very first start boots the cartridge once and takes about a minute; after that it's fast.)
  3. Your PC boxes and bag are emptied for the day, so it's just you and the six.
  4. The game notices when you win or lose and records it, along with how many Pokémon were still standing. One official Daily attempt; replays afterwards are practice.
  5. Unlimited: a fresh random six whenever you want. Play it, then hit New team for another.
  6. The game walks you in, talks to Cynthia and starts the battle for you; your keyboard only works during the battle.
