How to play

  

  
    
    
  1. Your six are already in your party: max IVs, 252/252 EVs, levels 76 with a level 78 ace, the same as Cynthia.
    2. 
    
  2. The game opens in Undella Town, then walks you into Caitlin's villa and accepts Cynthia's challenge for you.
    3. 
    
  3. Your PC boxes and bag are empty for the day, so it's just you and the six.
    4. 
    
  4. When the battle ends the result is recorded automatically. One official Daily attempt per day; Unlimited lets you keep going with new teams.
    5. 
    
  5. Your keyboard is locked until the battle starts and locks again when it ends; then you get your score.
